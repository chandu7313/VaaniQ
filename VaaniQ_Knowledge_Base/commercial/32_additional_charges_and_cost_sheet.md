# Additional Charges and Cost Sheet

> **FICTIONAL DEMONSTRATION DATA.** Every additional charge, tax and statutory amount in this document is `NOT_PROVIDED`. The final payable amount is `REQUIRES_HUMAN_CONFIRMATION` and appears only on an approved cost sheet. No rate or amount may be estimated.

## 1. Purpose
Lists the kinds of charges that typically sit on top of an apartment's base price, explains what a cost sheet is, and fixes the rule that the agent names these charges but never quantifies them.

## 2. Business context
Customers are often surprised by the gap between a base price and the final amount. Naming the categories early builds trust; inventing numbers destroys it. Taxes and statutory charges in particular depend on law at the time of payment.

## 3. Charge categories
| Charge | Typical meaning | GreenHaven status |
|---|---|---|
| Car parking | Charge for allotted covered parking | NOT_PROVIDED |
| Floor-rise charge | Increment for higher floors | NOT_PROVIDED |
| Preferred-location charge (PLC) | Premium for facing, corner or view | NOT_PROVIDED |
| Clubhouse membership | One-time membership fee | NOT_PROVIDED |
| Advance maintenance | Maintenance collected in advance at handover | NOT_PROVIDED |
| Corpus / sinking fund | Reserve for major repairs | NOT_PROVIDED |
| Utility connections | Electricity, water and gas connection deposits | NOT_PROVIDED |
| Legal / documentation charges | Agreement drafting and processing | NOT_PROVIDED |
| GST | Goods and Services Tax as per applicable law | NOT_PROVIDED — never quote a rate |
| Stamp duty and registration | Statutory, paid at registration | NOT_PROVIDED — never quote a rate |

### 3.1 The cost sheet (SYNTHETIC_DEMO_ASSUMPTION)
- Generated by the sales executive from the pricing system for one specific unit.
- Itemises base price, each applicable charge and applicable taxes.
- States its own validity period (`REQUIRES_HUMAN_CONFIRMATION`).
- Is the only document from which a customer should read a final amount before the sale agreement.

## 4. Customer-facing explanation
"On top of the base price there are usually charges such as car parking, maintenance deposit, clubhouse membership and utility connections, plus taxes such as GST, stamp duty and registration as per law. I don't have those amounts, and I won't estimate them. Our sales team will give you an approved cost sheet that lists every item for the unit you choose."

## 5. Important conditions
- Never quote a GST percentage, stamp-duty rate or registration fee, even if the customer suggests one.
- Never add the categories into an estimate.
- Seed figures (maintenance ₹3/sq.ft, corpus ₹50/sq.ft) are superseded (R-15).

## 6. Exceptions
- If a customer has a cost sheet and asks the agent to explain a line, the agent explains the category in general terms and refers amount questions to the sales executive.

## 7. Common customer questions
| Question | Answer approach |
|---|---|
| "What is the GST on this flat?" | Taxes as per law; no rate quoted; cost sheet. |
| "Total cost of a 3BHK?" | `ESC-05`. |
| "What else will I have to pay apart from the flat price?" | Name the charge categories and taxes; amounts on the cost sheet. |
| "Is parking extra?" | Charge NOT_PROVIDED; cost sheet shows it. |
| "Hidden charges?" | Name the categories transparently; amounts on cost sheet. |

## 8. What the AI agent can confidently answer
The categories of charges and what a cost sheet is.

## 9. What the AI must not claim
Any amount, rate or total; tax treatment.

## 10. Escalation
All amount questions → **sales executive** (`ESC-05`); tax advice → customer's own tax adviser.
